Solids, nets and views

A net is a flat pattern that can fold into a solid.

Grades 4–7

From flat to solid

A cube has 6 square faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices. Its net shows all six faces laid flat. Different nets can fold into the same cube, but not every arrangement of six squares works.

NUMBERS · BELOW ZERO

Meet negative numbers

Use a number line to see what positive and negative values mean.

Grades 5–7

Zero is the starting point

Positive numbers are to the right of zero. Negative numbers are to the left. The farther right a number lies, the greater it is. For example, −2 is greater than −5 because −2 is closer to zero.

Move the point

Drag the slider to place the point on the number line.

−3

−3 is negative because it is left of zero.
